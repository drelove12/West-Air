01 / OUR STORY

Born in Oregon.
Built for the Pacific.

Western Air is a new virtual airline project inspired by Portland, the Pacific Northwest, and the connections that aviation makes possible.

Our goal is to create a realistic, friendly virtual aviation community with thoughtful route planning, distinctive branding, immersive flight operations, and room for members to help shape the airline from day one.

Our main hub is Portland International Airport (PDX), with a proposed network stretching across North America, the Pacific Islands, Asia, and Oceania.

OUR PURPOSE ✦

Connect more than destinations.

Build a collaborative home for virtual pilots, designers, developers, planners, and aviation enthusiasts.

OUR VISION

Make PDX a vibrant virtual gateway to the Pacific through a growing network and an exceptional community experience.
